Behind every reliable AI system is carefully prepared data. As a Data Annotation Specialist, you'll label text, categorize items and evaluate written outputs for AI teams — work where precision matters more than speed.

You'll be trained and tested on each project's guidelines before production work begins.

## What you'll do

- Annotate and label data according to project guidelines and schemas
- Rate and compare written outputs for accuracy, relevance and tone
- Flag ambiguous items instead of labeling them inconsistently
- Meet accuracy thresholds measured against known-answer items
- Share feedback that helps improve guidelines

## What you'll bring

- **Strong English reading and writing** and an eye for nuance
- Patience with detailed, repetitive work — and the focus to keep it accurate
- Ability to follow complex written instructions exactly
- A reliable computer and internet connection

## Nice to have

- Previous annotation, transcription, editing or research experience
- Subject knowledge in areas such as finance, health, law or technology
- Additional languages
