Every support conversation is a chance for a customer to trust a company a little more. As a Customer Support Specialist, you'll work inside our clients' help desks, answer their customers by email and chat, and resolve issues — not just reply to them.

You'll learn each product through structured onboarding and supervised early tickets, so you start confident.

## What you'll do

- Respond to customer emails and live chats using the client's policies and tone
- Classify, tag and prioritize tickets so nothing sits unassigned
- Resolve order, account and product questions within agreed permissions
- Hand complex cases to the client's team with a clear summary
- Point out gaps in the knowledge base so answers improve over time

## What you'll bring

- **Excellent written English** — clear, warm and concise
- Patience and a genuine wish to solve problems
- Comfort learning new software tools quickly
- Organization and accuracy under a steady workload

## Nice to have

- Experience with help desk tools such as ticketing or live-chat systems
- Background in e-commerce, SaaS or subscription services
- Additional languages
