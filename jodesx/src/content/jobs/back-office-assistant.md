Some of the most valuable work in a business is the work nobody sees: accurate records, processed orders, documents filed where they belong. As a Back-Office Operations Assistant, you'll handle that work for international clients, following clear written procedures.

## What you'll do

- Enter and update records accurately across client systems
- Process orders, documents and routine requests within agreed turnaround times
- Check your work against source data before it's delivered
- Flag exceptions and missing information instead of guessing
- Help document procedures so every task is repeatable

## What you'll bring

- **High accuracy** and real attention to detail
- Confidence with spreadsheets and online business tools
- Good written English
- The discipline to follow procedures — and the sense to ask when something doesn't fit

## Nice to have

- Experience in administration, data entry, logistics or e-commerce operations
- Familiarity with spreadsheets beyond the basics (filters, lookups)
