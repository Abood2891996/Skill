Online platforms depend on people who can read context carefully and apply a policy the same way every time. As a Content Moderator at JoDesx, you review posts, profiles, images and comments for international platforms — and your decisions directly shape how safe those communities feel.

You will never be guessing. Every client engagement comes with written guidelines, a clear escalation path and a supervisor who reviews work with you.

## What you'll do

- Review user-generated content against the client's written policies
- Apply the agreed action — approve, remove, restrict or label — and record the reason
- Escalate ambiguous or sensitive cases with clear notes
- Take part in calibration sessions so the whole team reads guidelines the same way
- Flag recurring issues that suggest a policy needs clarifying

## What you'll bring

- **Fluent written English** and strong reading comprehension
- Sound judgment and the ability to stay consistent across hundreds of decisions
- Comfort following detailed written procedures
- Reliability with shift schedules and attention to detail
- Emotional resilience — some content can be difficult

## Nice to have

- Previous moderation, trust & safety or review experience
- Familiarity with online communities, marketplaces or social platforms
- Additional languages

## How we look after moderators

Shifts are planned with rotation and breaks in mind, and exposure to difficult material is discussed openly. Consistent judgment depends on people who are supported, not overexposed.
