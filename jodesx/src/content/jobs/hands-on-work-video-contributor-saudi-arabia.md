JoDesx is looking for skilled hands-on workers in Saudi Arabia to record their normal day-to-day work with a phone camera. The recordings are used to train and evaluate AI systems that learn how real work is done. You keep doing your usual job; the only difference is that you record it.

This is independent contract work, paid for every hour of video that passes quality review. We explain the exact rate, the recording app and how payment works when we speak with you.

## Who this is for

People doing real, hands-on work at a **commercial workplace** in Saudi Arabia, for example:

- Auto repair and maintenance workshops
- Air-conditioning (HVAC) and electrical work
- Construction and building maintenance
- Restaurant and commercial kitchen work
- Warehouses and stock handling
- Commercial cleaning

Recordings inside homes, and office or computer work, are not part of this project.

## What you'll do

- Record your real work as you normally do it, using the rear main camera of an eligible phone, in landscape orientation
- Keep your hands and the work area in view for most of the recording (at least 70% of the time), with the phone steady
- Record one complete task from start to finish, at a natural pace, with normal sound left on
- Upload recordings through the project app, usually every 15 to 20 minutes, and keep each recording between 1 and 60 minutes
- Follow the feedback our team shares with you to improve approval rates

## What you'll bring

- Hands-on work that you already do regularly at a commercial site in Saudi Arabia
- **Written permission from the owner of the workplace** to record there (or you are the owner)
- The legal ability to take on independent work in Saudi Arabia, for example a Saudi freelance permit (وثيقة العمل الحر) or as the business owner
- A recent smartphone from the eligible device list (we share it during screening)
- Care for other people's privacy: no customers, colleagues' faces, screens, documents, vehicle plates or other personal information in the recordings

## How it works

- **Screening:** a short call to check your type of work, your workplace permission and your phone.
- **Agreement:** a simple contractor agreement, including your consent to recording and to how the footage is used.
- **Trial week:** you record, we review together, and you get clear feedback on each video.
- **Payment:** for every approved hour, paid after each payment cycle. Rejected recordings are not paid, and every rejection comes with a reason.

You can write your application in Arabic. A CV is not required. Tell us about your work, where you do it and what phone you use.
