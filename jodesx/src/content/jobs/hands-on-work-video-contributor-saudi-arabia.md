**Your skills are already valuable. Now your everyday work can earn you more.**

JoDesx is looking for skilled hands-on workers in Saudi Arabia to film their normal working day with a phone. The videos help train AI systems to understand how real work is done, by real people. You don't change your job, your hours or your routine. You just record it, and get paid for every hour of video that passes review.

No CV, no interview marathon, no special experience in filming. We show you exactly how to record, and our team gives you feedback on every video.

## Any hands-on trade is welcome

If you work with your hands at a **commercial workplace** in Saudi Arabia, you're a fit. For example:

- Auto repair, tyres and car maintenance
- Air-conditioning (HVAC), electrical and plumbing work
- Construction, carpentry, welding, painting and building maintenance
- Restaurants, bakeries and commercial kitchens
- Warehouses, stock handling and packing
- Commercial cleaning, tailoring, barbering, crafts and workshops of every kind

Any real, skilled manual work counts. The only things we can't use: recordings inside homes, office or computer work, and repetitive assembly-line tasks.

## Why join

- **Extra income from work you already do:** paid for every approved hour of video
- **No change to your job:** same place, same hours, same tasks
- **Simple to start:** we explain the app and the recording rules in one short call
- **Clear feedback:** every rejected video comes with a reason, so you improve quickly
- **Arabic all the way:** you don't need English

## What you'll do

- Record your real work with the rear main camera of an eligible phone, held in landscape
- Keep your hands and the work area in view for most of the video (at least 70% of the time), with the phone steady
- Record one complete task from start to finish, at a natural pace, with sound on
- Upload through the project app, usually every 15 to 20 minutes; each recording runs 1 to 60 minutes

## What you'll need

- Hands-on work you do regularly at a commercial site in Saudi Arabia
- A recent smartphone from the eligible device list (we send it to you)
- Respect for other people's privacy: no customers, colleagues' faces, screens, documents, vehicle plates or other personal information in your videos

## How it works

1. **Apply** in two minutes on this page.
2. **Quick call:** we check your type of work, your workplace permission and your phone, and explain the exact rate.
3. **Sign a simple agreement**, including your consent to recording and how the videos are used.
4. **Start recording and earning.** Approved hours are paid after each payment cycle.

You can write your application in Arabic. A CV is not required, and the note about yourself is optional, but telling us your trade, where you work and what phone you use helps us reply faster.
