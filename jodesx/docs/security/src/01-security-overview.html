At a glance

  • Your data stays in your systems. Our teams work inside the tools you provide, through named accounts you control and can revoke at any time.
  • Every team member is screened (identity verification and a criminal-record certificate) and signs a confidentiality agreement before receiving any access.
  • Company-managed laptops only — encrypted, updated, protected and remotely manageable. No personal devices are used for client work.
  • Multi-factor authentication and a password manager for every account, with access limited to what each role needs.
  • Security incidents affecting your data are reported to you within 24 hours of being confirmed.

We provide content moderation, customer support, back-office operations and AI data operations, with teams working remotely or on-site at our offices in Jordan. This document summarises the security and data-protection controls that apply to every engagement. Engagement-specific requirements are agreed in writing with each client.

2Our principles

  • Least data: client data remains in client systems. We do not copy it into JoDesx systems unless this is required for the service and agreed in writing.
  • Least access: each person receives only the access their role requires, for as long as they need it.
  • Accountable people: screened, trained team members who sign confidentiality obligations.
  • Documented and reviewed: controls are written down, checked and reviewed at least once a year.

3People

  • Pre-employment screening before any access to client systems: identity verification and a criminal-record (non-conviction) certificate, together with reference checks where available.
  • Confidentiality: every employee and contractor signs a confidentiality agreement and acknowledges the JoDesx Information Security Policy before starting work. Client-specific NDAs are signed where requested.
  • Training: security and data-protection training at onboarding and at least once a year, plus client-specific training on your policies and tools.
  • Wellbeing in content moderation: shifts are planned with rotation and breaks to support consistent, careful judgment.

4Access control

  • Named accounts: each team member uses an individual account in your systems. Shared logins are not used unless you require them, and are then stored and shared only through our password manager.
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is enforced on all JoDesx systems and enabled on client systems wherever they support it.
  • Password manager: credentials are stored in a business password manager — never in documents, chats or email.
  • Least privilege: we request only the permissions needed for the agreed scope.
  • Access reviews: access lists are reviewed with you at least every quarter.
  • Same-day removal: when someone leaves the engagement or the company, JoDesx access is removed the same day and we ask you to revoke theirs in your systems.

5Devices

Client work is performed only on company-owned laptops managed by JoDesx. Each device has:

  • full-disk encryption (BitLocker or FileVault);
  • automatic operating-system and security updates;
  • active endpoint protection (anti-malware);
  • automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity;
  • a standard (non-administrator) user account for daily work;
  • enrolment in device management, allowing remote lock and wipe if a device is lost or stolen;
  • no use of removable storage (USB drives) for client data.

Personal computers, personal phones and personal cloud storage are not used to access or store client data.

6Working environment

Office

  • Access to work areas is restricted to authorised staff; visitors are recorded and accompanied.
  • Clean-desk and clear-screen practices: screens are locked when unattended and no client information is left on paper.

Remote work

  • Work from a private space where screens cannot be seen by others; no client work in public places.
  • A password-protected home network; no public or open Wi-Fi for client work.
  • No photographs, screenshots or recordings of client systems unless required by the agreed procedure.

7Data handling

  • Client data is not copied to personal email, messaging apps, personal storage or local files unless the agreed procedure requires it.
  • AI tools: client data is never entered into public or third-party AI tools unless you approve it in writing.
  • Printing client information is not permitted unless agreed in writing.
  • End of engagement: access is removed, any client data held by JoDesx is returned or deleted, and deletion is confirmed to you in writing.

8JoDesx corporate systems

  • Business email and productivity tools (Zoho) with MFA enforced, and email authentication (SPF, DKIM and DMARC) on the jodesx.com domain.
  • Our website (www.jodesx.com) is served over HTTPS only, with strict security headers. It uses no tracking cookies or analytics, and its forms collect only the information needed to reply.
  • Administrative access to corporate systems is limited to JoDesx management.

9Incident response

  1. Team members report any suspected incident — a lost device, a suspicious message, accidental exposure of data — to JoDesx management immediately.
  2. We contain the incident: revoke access, lock or wipe devices, and preserve relevant records.
  3. If your data or systems may be affected, we notify you within 24 hours of confirming the incident, with what we know, what we have done and what we recommend.
  4. We cooperate fully with your investigation and complete a written review with corrective actions.

10Business continuity

  • Backup internet connectivity and power protection for office operations.
  • The ability to continue work remotely on company devices if an office becomes unavailable.
  • Coverage and escalation contacts agreed with each client during setup.

11Contracts, subcontracting and certification

  • We sign client NDAs and data processing agreements (DPAs), including terms required under the GDPR where applicable.
  • Client work is performed by JoDesx staff. We do not subcontract client work to third parties without your written approval.
  • JoDesx does not currently hold ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification. Our controls follow widely used security practices, and we are happy to complete your security questionnaire or discuss specific requirements.
  • This document and the underlying policy are reviewed at least once a year and whenever our operations change.
