Why this matters

Our clients trust us with access to their systems, their customers and their data. One careless action can harm a client and end an engagement. These rules protect the clients, the company and you. If a rule is unclear, or you think something is unsafe, ask your manager — asking is always the right choice.

1Scope

This policy applies to every JoDesx employee and contractor, on-site or remote, and to all JoDesx devices, accounts and client systems you can access. Breaking it may lead to disciplinary action, up to termination of employment or contract.

2Confidentiality

  • Everything you see in client systems is confidential: customer details, content, data, documents and procedures.
  • Never discuss client work, client names or what you see at work outside JoDesx — including on social media — unless the client has publicly agreed.
  • Your confidentiality obligations continue after you leave JoDesx.

3Accounts and passwords

  • Use only the accounts given to you. Never share your account or use someone else's.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA) on every account where it is available. Use an authenticator app, not SMS, where possible.
  • Store all work passwords in the JoDesx password manager (Bitwarden). Never write passwords on paper, in documents, chats or emails.
  • Use a unique, long password for each account. Let the password manager generate it.
  • Never approve an MFA request you did not start. Report it immediately.

4Your company laptop

  • Use only your JoDesx laptop for client work. Never use a personal computer or phone to access client systems or data.
  • Do not turn off encryption, updates, anti-malware or device management, and do not install software without approval.
  • Lock your screen whenever you step away (Windows: Win + L; Mac: Ctrl + Cmd + Q).
  • Do not lend the laptop to anyone, including family members.
  • Never leave the laptop unattended in a car or public place.
  • Do not connect USB drives or other storage devices for client work.
  • Report a lost or stolen laptop to your manager immediately, at any hour.

5Handling client data

  • Keep client data inside client systems. Do not copy it to email, chat apps, personal storage, local files or paper unless the written procedure tells you to.
  • Never paste client data into ChatGPT or any other public AI tool, translation website or online service, unless your manager confirms the client approved it in writing.
  • Do not take photos, screenshots or recordings of client systems unless the procedure requires it.
  • Do not print client information.
  • Use client data only for the task you were given — never out of curiosity.

6Phone calls

  • Always verify the caller exactly as the client procedure requires before discussing any account information.
  • Never write down, type into notes or repeat aloud a caller’s full card number or security code. Use only the payment process the client has approved.
  • Never record calls yourself, on any device. Recording is handled only by the approved phone system.
  • For outbound calls, call only the approved lists, within the approved hours, and respect any request not to be called again.
  • Use your headset; never take client calls on speakerphone or in public.

7Where you work

In the office

  • Do not let anyone follow you into restricted areas. Accompany your visitors.
  • Keep your desk clear of client information and lock your screen when you leave.

Working remotely

  • Work in a private space where others cannot see your screen or hear client calls.
  • Use your password-protected home network. Never use public or open Wi-Fi (cafés, malls, hotels) for client work.
  • Do not work on client tasks in public places.

8Email, messages and phishing

  • Be suspicious of unexpected messages asking for passwords, codes, payments or urgent action — even if they look like they come from a manager or client.
  • Do not open unexpected attachments or links. If in doubt, ask.
  • Use only approved JoDesx and client channels for work communication.

9Reporting incidents

Report immediately to your manager or security@jodesx.com if:

  • your laptop is lost or stolen;
  • you clicked a suspicious link, entered a password on a suspicious page or approved an MFA request by mistake;
  • client data was sent to the wrong person or exposed;
  • you notice anything else that seems unsafe.

You will not be blamed for reporting honestly and quickly. Hiding an incident is far more serious than the incident itself.

10Leaving a client or JoDesx

  • Return your laptop and any company equipment on your last day.
  • Do not keep, copy or forward any client or company information.
  • Your access will be removed the same day.

11Content moderation wellbeing

If you work on content moderation, use the breaks and rotation built into your schedule. If content affects you, tell your supervisor — this is part of doing the job well, not a weakness.
