DIncident response — first hour

  • Record what happened, when, who reported it and which client(s) may be affected
  • Contain: disable affected accounts, change passwords, lock or wipe the device via MDM
  • Preserve evidence (messages, logs, screenshots) — do not delete anything
  • Decide whether client data or systems may be affected
  • If yes: notify the client within 24 hours of confirming — what happened, what was done, what is recommended
  • Personal data involved: assess obligations under the Jordanian data protection law and GDPR (with legal advice)
  • Within 2 weeks: written review — cause, fix, and changes to prevent it happening again
