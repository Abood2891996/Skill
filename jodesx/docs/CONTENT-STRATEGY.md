# Content strategy

All site copy lives in `src/content/` (see "Editing content" in the README). This document explains the thinking behind it so future edits stay coherent.

## 1. Audience

Operations, support, trust & safety and data leads at international businesses — and the founders/COOs of smaller companies doing those jobs themselves. They are evaluating risk: *Will handing this over create more work than it saves?*

## 3. Messaging hierarchy

1. **Category:** business operations and outsourcing — four services.
2. **Differentiator:** an operations partner that designs how the work runs, not a supplier of people.
3. **Mechanism:** written scope → documented procedures → quality review → reporting.
4. **Outcome:** operational capacity extended; internal teams freed for higher-value work.

Positioning line: *Operational support, intelligently connected.* (footer, metadata)
Home headline: *Operations, structured around your business.*
Closing line: *Let's make your next operation work better.*

## 5. Copywriting principles

- **Show, don't assert.** Replace adjectives with mechanisms ("a portion of decisions is re-reviewed" instead of "high quality").
- **Banned phrases:** world-class, best-in-class, innovative solutions, unmatched, passionate about excellence, your success is our success, customer satisfaction is our priority, cutting-edge, seamless.
- **CTAs say what happens:** "Start an inquiry", "Discuss this challenge", "Send inquiry", "Compare all services". The same action keeps the same name across the flow.
- **Errors explain the fix:** "Enter a phone number including the country code, like +44 20 1234 5678."
- **Sentence case** everywhere; no all-caps labels.
- **US English** spelling; plain words; short paragraphs; one idea per sentence.
- **Never flatter, never pressure.** No urgency, no scarcity, no pop-ups.

## 6. Credibility rules (non-negotiable)

The site contains **no** client names or logos, testimonials, case studies, statistics, employee counts, years in business, certifications, awards, partnerships or office addresses — because none have been confirmed. Credibility is built from process transparency instead.

Claims that *are* made and the basis for each:

| Claim | Basis |
| --- | --- |
| Based in Jordan, working internationally | Brief |
| Jordan is UTC+3; overlaps European/Gulf hours and the start of the North American day | Geographic fact (Jordan has used UTC+3 year-round since 2022) |
| Remote employees or contracted specialists, depending on engagement | Brief (business model) |
| Teams work remotely or on-site at JoDesx offices in Jordan, as the client prefers | Owner, 17 Nov 2026 |
| Engagement models (dedicated / managed / flexible), pilot-first approach | Proposed operating model — **needs business confirmation** |
| Language coverage confirmed per engagement | Deliberately non-committal |
