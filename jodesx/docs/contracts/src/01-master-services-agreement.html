How this agreement works

  • This agreement (MSA) holds the terms that stay the same for every engagement: confidentiality, data protection, payment, liability, termination.
  • A Statement of Work (SOW) describes each engagement: the service, scope, team, hours, location, reporting and fees. A client can have several SOWs under one MSA. Template: Schedule 1.
  • The Data Processing Addendum (DPA) sets the rules for personal data JoDesx handles on the client’s behalf. Outline: Schedule 2.

JoDesx and the Client are each a “Party” and together the “Parties”.

1Definitions

  • Services — the services described in a signed SOW.
  • SOW — a Statement of Work signed by both Parties that refers to this Agreement.
  • Team — the employees and individual contracted specialists JoDesx assigns to perform the Services.
  • Client Materials — information, data, content, documents, procedures, systems access and tools the Client provides.
  • Client Data — any data, including personal data, that JoDesx receives or accesses on the Client’s behalf.
  • Deliverables — work product created by the Team specifically for the Client under an SOW (for example tickets handled, labelled data, reports).
  • Confidential Information — as defined in clause 9.

2Structure and order of precedence

  1. Each engagement is described in an SOW. An SOW is binding only once signed by both Parties.
  2. If documents conflict, the following order applies: (a) the DPA, for anything about personal data; (b) this Agreement; (c) the SOW, unless the SOW expressly states that it overrides a named clause of this Agreement.

3Services

  1. JoDesx will perform the Services described in each SOW, which may include: call center operations (inbound, outbound, technical support, telesales); customer support; content moderation; back-office operations; and AI data operations (human data work for AI teams — JoDesx does not build AI models).
  2. Services are delivered in Arabic and/or English, as stated in the SOW.
  3. The Team works remotely or on-site at JoDesx offices in Jordan, as stated in the SOW. Work is performed within the Client’s systems where the SOW requires it.
  4. JoDesx will perform the Services with reasonable skill and care, following the documented procedures agreed with the Client.

4Client responsibilities

The Client will, on time and at its own cost:

  1. provide the Client Materials, systems access, accounts and training content reasonably needed for the Services;
  2. name a contact person with authority to give instructions and approve procedures;
  3. ensure that its instructions, policies, scripts and content guidelines comply with the law that applies to its business and its customers;
  4. for outbound calling and telesales: provide contact lists that were obtained lawfully, with any consent the target country requires, and tell JoDesx of any calling-time, do-not-call or recording rules that apply;
  5. for call recording or monitoring: decide whether calls are recorded and provide the notice wording its customers must hear;
  6. for content moderation: provide written content policies and an escalation route for content that may require reporting to authorities.

JoDesx is not responsible for delays or failures caused by the Client not meeting these responsibilities.

Note for counsel: clause 4.4–4.5 places responsibility for calling and recording rules of the client’s market on the client, because those rules depend on where the client’s customers are. Please confirm what JoDesx itself must comply with under Jordanian law, including any licensing by the Telecommunications Regulatory Commission (TRC) for call center activity.

5Team and supervision

  1. The Team are employees or individual contracted specialists of JoDesx. They are organised, supervised and paid by JoDesx and are not employees of the Client.
  2. Before receiving access to Client Data, each Team member passes JoDesx screening (identity check and non-conviction certificate) and signs a confidentiality agreement and the JoDesx Information Security Policy.
  3. JoDesx may replace a Team member, giving the Client reasonable notice and handover. The Client may ask, with reasons, for a Team member to be removed from its account; JoDesx will not unreasonably refuse.
  4. JoDesx will not engage another company to perform the Services without the Client’s prior written consent.

  1. Service levels, quality measures and reporting are set out in the SOW. If the SOW sets none, JoDesx will provide [weekly / monthly] written reporting on volume and quality.
  2. [Service credits apply only where an SOW expressly sets them.]
  3. The Parties will meet [monthly] to review performance and agree improvements.

7Changes

Either Party may request a change to an SOW (scope, team size, hours, languages). A change is binding only once both Parties sign a written change, including any effect on fees and timing.

8Fees, invoicing and payment

  1. Fees are set out in each SOW, in [USD].
  2. JoDesx invoices [monthly in advance / monthly in arrears]. Invoices are payable within [30] days of the invoice date by bank transfer to the account stated on the invoice.
  3. Fees exclude taxes. [Treatment of Jordanian general sales tax on exported services, and any withholding tax in the Client’s country, to be confirmed by counsel and JoDesx’s accountant.] Bank charges are paid by [the Client].
  4. If an undisputed invoice is more than [15] days overdue, JoDesx may, after written notice, suspend the Services until payment is made. [Late-payment interest: to be confirmed by counsel.]
  5. The Client must raise any dispute about an invoice in writing within [10] days of receiving it and pay the undisputed part on time.
  6. JoDesx may review fees once a year with [60] days’ written notice; a review takes effect only for SOWs that renew after the notice.

9Confidentiality

  1. “Confidential Information” means non-public information one Party discloses to the other in connection with this Agreement, including Client Data, business information, procedures, pricing and the terms of this Agreement.
  2. The receiving Party will use Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement, protect it with at least reasonable care, and disclose it only to people who need it for this Agreement and are bound by confidentiality obligations.
  3. These obligations do not apply to information that is or becomes public through no fault of the receiving Party, was lawfully known to it already, or must be disclosed by law (with prompt notice to the other Party where lawful).
  4. JoDesx will not name the Client publicly or use its name or logo as a reference without the Client’s prior written consent.
  5. These obligations continue for [5] years after this Agreement ends, and without time limit for personal data and trade secrets.

10Data protection

  1. For Client Data that is personal data, the Client is the controller and JoDesx is the processor. JoDesx processes it only on the Client’s documented instructions, as set out in the DPA (Schedule 2).
  2. Each Party complies with the data-protection law that applies to it, including the Personal Data Protection Law of Jordan and, where applicable, the EU or UK GDPR.
  3. JoDesx will notify the Client within 24 hours of confirming a security incident that affects Client Data, and will cooperate with the Client’s response.

11Security

  1. JoDesx will apply the security measures described in its Information Security Policy, including: company-owned, encrypted and centrally managed devices; multi-factor authentication; a company password manager; least-privilege access with quarterly access reviews; and same-day removal of access when a Team member leaves.
  2. The Client may require additional measures in the SOW. Additional measures that add cost will be agreed in writing.
  3. JoDesx does not hold ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification. Nothing in this Agreement states or implies that it does.

12Intellectual property

  1. The Client keeps all rights in the Client Materials and Client Data.
  2. On payment of the related fees, the Client owns the Deliverables. [JoDesx assigns to the Client any rights it holds in the Deliverables.]
  3. JoDesx keeps its own general know-how, methods, templates and tools that are not specific to the Client.
  4. JoDesx will not use Client Data or Deliverables to train, build or improve any AI model, or for any purpose other than the Services.

13Warranties

  1. Each Party confirms that it has the authority to enter into this Agreement.
  2. JoDesx warrants that the Services will be performed with reasonable skill and care. If they are not, JoDesx will, as the Client’s first remedy, re-perform the affected Services at no extra cost.
  3. Other than as stated in this Agreement, no warranties are given, to the extent the law allows.

15Limitation of liability

  1. Neither Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss, or for loss of profit, revenue or goodwill.
  2. Each Party’s total liability under this Agreement in any 12-month period is limited to [the fees paid or payable under the relevant SOW in the 12 months before the claim].
  3. These limits do not apply to: unpaid fees; breach of confidentiality; [breach of data-protection obligations — or a separate, higher cap]; fraud; or liability that cannot be limited by law.

Note for the owner: clients often negotiate clause 15. Decide in advance the highest cap JoDesx can accept, and check with an insurance broker whether professional-liability or cyber insurance is available and affordable before agreeing to a higher cap.

16Term and termination

  1. This Agreement starts on the date it is signed and continues until terminated. Each SOW has its own term.
  2. Either Party may terminate an SOW for convenience with [60] days’ written notice[, after a minimum term of [3] months].
  3. Either Party may terminate this Agreement or an SOW immediately by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach and does not remedy it within [30] days of notice, or becomes insolvent.
  4. On termination: the Client pays for Services performed up to the end date; JoDesx returns or deletes Client Data as instructed (clause 6 of Schedule 2); and JoDesx gives reasonable help to hand over the work, [at its standard rates].
  5. Clauses 9, 10, 12, 15, 17, 19 and 20 survive termination.

17Non-solicitation

During each SOW and for [12] months after it ends, the Client will not directly hire or engage a Team member who worked on that SOW without JoDesx’s written consent. [If it does, the Client pays a placement fee of [___].] General job advertisements are not solicitation.

Note for counsel: please confirm enforceability under Jordanian law and the Client’s likely jurisdiction.

18Force majeure

Neither Party is liable for delay or failure caused by events beyond its reasonable control (for example natural disasters, war, government action, widespread power, internet or telecommunications outages). The affected Party will notify the other promptly and take reasonable steps to continue the Services. If the event lasts more than [30] days, either Party may terminate the affected SOW by written notice.

19Governing law and disputes

  1. This Agreement is governed by [the laws of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan].

  3. If unresolved, the dispute is settled by [the competent courts of Amman, Jordan / arbitration under the rules of ___, seated in ___, in English].

S2Schedule 2 — Data Processing Addendum (outline)

Note for counsel: this is an outline. Clients in the EU or UK will usually ask for their own DPA or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers to Jordan; JoDesx should review those rather than insist on this text.

  1. Details of processing: subject matter, duration, nature and purpose, types of personal data, and categories of data subjects — set out in each SOW.
  2. Instructions: JoDesx processes personal data only on the Client’s documented instructions, and tells the Client if it believes an instruction breaks the law.
  3. Confidentiality: only Team members bound by confidentiality obligations have access, on a need-to-know basis.
  4. Security: the measures in clause 11 of the Agreement and the JoDesx Information Security Policy.
  5. Sub-processors: JoDesx lists any third-party services it uses to process Client Data in the SOW, and gives the Client [30] days’ notice and the right to object before adding a new one.
  6. Return and deletion: at the end of the Services, JoDesx returns or deletes Client Data, as the Client chooses, within [30] days, unless the law requires it to keep a copy.
  7. Incidents: notice to the Client within 24 hours of confirming an incident affecting Client Data, with the information the Client needs to meet its own obligations.
  8. Assistance: JoDesx helps the Client respond to data-subject requests and regulator enquiries that relate to the Services.
  9. Audits: JoDesx provides information needed to show compliance and allows audits on [30] days’ notice, no more than [once a year], during business hours, at the Client’s cost.
  10. International transfers: Client Data is accessed from Jordan. Where the Client’s law requires a transfer mechanism, the Parties sign it as an annex.
